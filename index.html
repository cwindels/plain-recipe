Plain Recipe

One click on any recipe page and the life story, pop-ups and ads disappear. You get the ingredients and the steps, nothing else. Free, runs in your own browser, sends nothing anywhere.

Set up once

Add the button to your bookmarks

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows, ⌘+Shift+B on Mac.
  2. Plain Recipe
    Drag the green button up onto your bookmarks bar. If dragging doesn't work, copy the code, make a new bookmark named “Plain Recipe”, and paste the code into its URL field.
  3. Open any recipe (AllRecipes, NYT Cooking, Serious Eats, food blogs) and click Plain Recipe. Tap ingredients to tick them off and steps to cross them out. Use Print on the card to print just the recipe, and press Esc to go back to the page.
On a phone or tablet

Bookmark any page, then edit that bookmark: rename it “Plain Recipe” and replace its address with the code below. On a recipe page, open your bookmarks and tap it. In Chrome on Android you can also type “Plain Recipe” in the address bar and pick the bookmark.

Paste a page's source

No bookmark needed: on the recipe page press Ctrl+U (⌘+Option+U on Mac) to view its source, select all, copy, and paste it here. The box below starts with an example page.
